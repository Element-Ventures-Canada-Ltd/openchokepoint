# SOI Sovereign-Control Rating Criteria, v1.0 (published)

Status: published 4 Nov 2026. Owner: Element Ventures (Canada) Ltd. Product: SOI (Sovereign Operating Intelligence).

## What is rated
- One actor at one supply-chain node. The question: can the nation obtain this node's output on its own terms and timelines?
- Jurisdiction of control, not residency: control is set by the ultimate parent.
- Sovereign ceiling: an entity cannot rate above its ultimate parent's jurisdiction.
- Inputs are rated at their own nodes. A rating does not absorb upstream dependencies (no double counting); the map shows those through dependency links.

## The scale
| Rating | Definition |
|---|---|
| SC1 | Nationally controlled, with two or more qualified nationally controlled sources for the node, or assured national access (e.g., Crown ownership or stockpile). Replaceable on national timelines. |
| SC2 | Nationally controlled; a single qualified source, or not yet proven at the required scale. |
| SC3 | Nationally controlled, with a material foreign dependency in its own operation: design authority or production abroad, interim or revocable tenure, or foreign export-control consent needed to operate. |
| SC4 | Allied-controlled, including a foreign-parent subsidiary operating in the nation (sovereign ceiling). No adversary control or dominance. |
| SC5 | Adversary-controlled, adversary-dominated supply, or subject to an active adversary export-control regime. |

## Flags (shown beside the rating; they do not change it)
- **Sole source**: the only qualified source in evidence for the node.
- **No substitute in class**: no alternative of the required class exists or is in development on the planning horizon.

## Statuses that are not ratings
- **No evidence yet**: not assessed. Unknown, never low risk.
- **None identified**: an analyst screen found no national supplier.
- **Unverified control**: control not confirmed by a dated source. Excluded from national counts until verified.

## Evidence
- Every displayed rating needs a dated, linked source. A record without one shows as "citation needed" and is not rated on screen.
- Grades: C confirmed (primary source), R reported (credible secondary), I inferred (analytical judgement), S speculative (forward-looking).

## Background ratings
- Government bodies (the client, the sponsor, regulators, agencies) and commercial partners of Element Ventures are assessed but kept in the background. They are not displayed, and are callable by the owner when required. Partner relationships are disclosed whenever a background rating is called.

## Chokepoint score
For each node: Score = Volume band × Exposure × Control gap.
- Volume band (1–4) from annual units or transactions on current programmes: 1 = under 10; 2 = 10–99; 3 = 100–999; 4 = 1,000 or more. Missing volume = 1, and is flagged.
- Exposure (1–4): Low, Medium, High, Extreme (generic layer).
- Control gap (1–5): best available rating at the node (SC1 = 1 … SC5 = 5); None identified = 5; No evidence yet = 4.

## Limits
Ratings are illustrative analytical judgements from public sources. They are not findings about any company, its compliance or its conduct. Mapping covers part and supplier identity only.
